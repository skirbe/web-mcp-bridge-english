Welcome to Web MCP Bridge

Connect ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek with your local tools (MCP). Just follow these steps; the wizard updates itself as you go.

  1. 1

    Install the launcher

    The browser cannot start programs by itself. The launcher is a small process that you install once, starts with Windows and lets you start, stop and restart the bridge from this extension.

    1. Open the extension folder and go into server.
    2. Double-click install.cmd and wait for it to say “Done”.

    Prefer the terminal? python server/install_service.py

  2. 2

    Start the bridge

    The bridge is the program that exposes your MCP servers. You can control it from here or from the extension icon.

  3. 3

    Your MCP servers

    They are detected automatically from server/servers.json. Enable them (maximum 5) and choose their permissions from the extension icon.

    • 4

      Try it in your favorite chat

      Open one of these sites with the extension active and ask something that uses your tools.
